EDITOR’S NOTE

Couldn't find a note taking app that looked the way I wanted. Everything felt either way too corporate or packed with features I'd never use. I grew up using stuff like Photoshop and VS Code, so I ended up stealing that whole vibe and turning it into a place for notes instead.

I never sat down with a big plan for this. I just wanted to see what a notes app would look like if I built it my way. One feature turned into another, a few late nights disappeared, and it slowly became the website your looking at now.

“Honestly, I didn't have anything better to do.”

- anam razzaque

Most note-taking apps get the job done. I just wanted one that looked and felt a little different without making everything more complicated.
